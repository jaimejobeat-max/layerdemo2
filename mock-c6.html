Factories, a church, houses — rewritten

Layer Studios began in Seongsu-dong in 2009. A 1,500m² steel factory, a substation from 1939, a sixty-year-old church, a house next door to the Leeum Museum. We kept the bones of buildings that had outlived their use, and left the light and the ceilings alone.

Eleven spaces across Seoul, open to photo and film shoots as well as exhibitions, pop-ups, performances and brand events. What happens inside is up to you.

Layer Studios are rental studios in Seongsu-dong, Yongsan and Mapo, Seoul. Eleven spaces made from a former steel factory, a substation, a church and houses, for photo and film shoots, exhibitions, pop-ups, performances and brand events. Working with Seoul's creators since 2009, Layer keeps the time and grain of each building and lets the space speak for itself.
